PULSE (A typing marathon)

is stuck in my head and has been for days and days. I find that songs
stuck in heads usually has something to do with something not yet resolved,
and will affect the outcome of current projects. I've made a video edit chart
based on the song. I made a head stall to hold the camera, and Dan has
videoed for me where I couldn't.
