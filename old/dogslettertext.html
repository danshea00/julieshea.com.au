The Dog's Letter

At 86 George Street, Sydney, on 11.11.11, a silent question was answered regarding
the extension of the "Dog Pour" series where a dog's chase is reflected in a teapot.

A bird activated the "R" key on a wireless keyboard.
"R" is the dog's letter.
"R" is the eighteenth letter of the alphabet.
Eighteen is a spiritual number of Chai (living) in Judaism.
This video is a compilation of 54 small video sections.
The background sound to the selected "R" sounds is from "Lechaim" - ("To Life")
- the Jewish celebratory song.
Chai is also tea.
Tea draws in the pot.
Art draws from life.

Many thanks to serendipity, COFA, NIEA, Leanne Berelowitz and to those who
participated in this video.
